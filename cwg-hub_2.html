CONVERSATIONS WITH GOD

CWG · Wisdom for the AI Age

God's wisdom. Humanity's trials. Now amplified by AI — how does it all serve us now?
Artificial intelligence is not just a new tool. It is the largest civilizational rupture in human history — dismantling the systems, identities, and stories we built our lives around. The Conversations with God trilogy explored the deeper questions beneath that rupture before most of us knew it was coming. We read it now — one chapter at a time — through podcast, debate, music, and story.

Does the wisdom of the past still measure up? For most of us who have sat with these three axioms — We Are All One, There Is Enough, There Is Nothing We Have To Do — the answer is mostly yes. If you find yourself nodding, then join us: exploring how advanced AI, aligned with that foundation, can produce genuinely better results in our daily lives, work, and becoming.
